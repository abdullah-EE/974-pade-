# 974 Padel Premium UI

Use this skill when editing the 974 Padel Expo app UI.

## Product Standard
974 Padel should feel like a premium Qatar padel discovery, open-game, challenge, and verified ranking prototype.

Prioritize:
- Play-first court discovery.
- Image-led padel venue presentation.
- Human local-state interactions.
- Qatar maroon, pearl, white, charcoal, and restrained accent maroon.
- Realistic Qatar/Gulf/expat player and court content.

Avoid:
- Tennis, football, basketball, volleyball, kids sports, and unrelated stock imagery.
- Fake filler names, repeated copy, repeated images, or raw date strings.
- Generic SaaS card stacks, crowded stats, low-contrast gray text, random blue/gold/purple palettes, and triangle/default icons.
- Dead primary buttons.

## Screen Rules
- Play must sell the app quickly with a large padel hero carousel, search, filters, Available Tonight, Open Games Near You, and Top Courts This Week.
- Court detail should feel like a venue page with hero image, slots, amenities, players, recent ranked matches, map placeholder, booking, submit, and challenge actions.
- Rankings should feel alive with a podium, movers, filters, verified badges, and player-detail navigation.
- Submit must be a usable four-step local flow: Court, Players, Score, Proof + Review.
- Challenges must support join, accept, decline, sent, incoming, and accepted states locally.
- Profile should be premium and focused, not crowded.

## QA
Before claiming done, confirm typecheck/build health, exactly five tabs, working local interactions, human dates, relevant imagery/fallbacks, and no generic/fake content.
