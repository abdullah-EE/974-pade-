# 974 Padel Product Skill

Use this skill when editing mock data, product flows, court discovery, people search, friends, challenges, rankings, or profile.

## Product Positioning
974 Padel is a Qatar-first padel network. Its speciality is rankings plus social/player networking, not just court listings.

The core loop is:
Find court -> view availability-style slots -> book externally -> create/open ranked match -> add/challenge players -> submit proof -> verify result -> rank/status changes -> rematch.

## Venue Rules
- Use real Qatar padel venues where possible.
- Booking is external for now. Do not pretend a booking succeeded inside the app.
- Each court needs distinct area, description, image, availability status, slots, amenities, players, matches, and booking link.
- Full slots cannot be selected.

## People and Friends
- Use realistic Qatar/Gulf/expat names.
- Every player needs level, rating, record, movement, favorite court, form/streak, verified status, and avatar treatment.
- People search must support name/username and filters by level, area, or club.
- Users can add/remove friends locally.
- Friends list should show status such as online, playing tonight, available, or recently active.

## Challenges
Challenges must support:
- opponent or open invite
- court
- date/time
- level
- privacy: Public, Friends only, Private invite
- optional note
- local status updates: Sent, Incoming, Accepted, Declined

Created challenges should appear immediately in Sent Challenges or Open Games. Accept, decline, and join must update local state.

## Rankings and Profile
Rankings should feel like athlete status:
- top 3 podium
- weekly movers
- top 100
- working filters
- player detail actions: challenge, compare, add friend

Profile should show the local account, rank, rating, record, win rate, recent form, streak, badges, friends, favorite courts, and my challenges.

## Coaching, Video, Wallet
- Coaching and video are internal modules inside the five tabs, never new tabs.
- Coaches need specialties, court/area, price placeholder, slots, and request-session local state.
- Videos need thumbnail, title, creator, tag, duration, views, like/save state, and a detail sheet.
- Wallet/customization is local only: credits, cosmetic previews, locked state, premium placeholder, no real payments.
